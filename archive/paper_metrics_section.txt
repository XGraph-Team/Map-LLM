\subsection{System Performance}

We evaluated LLM-to-Map across seven performance dimensions to validate its suitability for interactive demonstrations and real-time resilience analysis.

\noindent\textbf{Response Time.} System achieves 1.87s response time for 95% of queries (n=100, avg=1.17s).

\noindent\textbf{LLM Accuracy.} LLM correctly interprets 87.0% of location queries with typos (n=100, typo distance <=2 Levenshtein edits).

\noindent\textbf{Concurrent Users.} Supports 10 concurrent users without performance degradation (P95 response time <3s, success rate >95%).

\noindent\textbf{Simulation Performance.} Handles 95+ vehicles simultaneously without lag (<4 FPS drop from 60 baseline, maintaining real-time visualization).

\noindent\textbf{Task Completion.} 98.0% task completion rate for infrastructure control commands (n=10 users, 5 task types).

\noindent\textbf{Hardware Requirements.} Demo runs on standard hardware (28-core CPU, 63.7GB RAM), consuming avg 31.8% CPU, 2.66GB memory, ~1.58 MB/s bandwidth.

\noindent\textbf{Scenario Timing.} Average scenario completion: 4.0 minutes (full 3-scenario demonstration: ~12.1 minutes).
